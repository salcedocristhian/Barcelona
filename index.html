      Where each measurement comes from

      Building floor plans: measured from NV5 CAD drawings (scale 3/16″ = 1′-0″). Length 201′-7″, depth 78′-4″, 25′-0″ units and a 5′-0″ corridor. All four buildings are identical.

      Orientation: from the north arrow on each NV5 sheet.

      Plan location of each mark: from the NV5 drawing for its building; the sheet is listed in the details.

      Heights: estimated from photos (floor-to-floor 9′-4″, eave 18′-8″, roof 22′-4″). No drawing gives heights, so each mark’s height is approximate.

      Site, clubhouse, pool and parking: aerial view calibrated to the measured building length. Accuracy is within a few feet.

      Life-safety devices: from the typical diagram in the phasing plan, which is not to scale. Assumed identical in all four buildings and on both floors.

      Windows, entrances and finishes: from photos of Building 100, applied to all.
