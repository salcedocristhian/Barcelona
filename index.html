      Where each measurement comes from

      Building floor plans: measured from NV5 CAD drawings (3/16″ = 1′-0″). Length 201′-7″, depth 78′-4″, 25′-0″ units and a 5′-0″ corridor. All four buildings are identical; the 750 sq ft listing for a 1-bedroom unit agrees with the measured 25′ × 28′ unit plus balcony.

      Site: checked against the Google satellite views with the 50 ft scale bar (building depth 78′ in both). Angled stalls along the drives, perpendicular stalls along SE 14th St.

      Clubhouse, pool and courtyard: from the aerial and listing photos: arcades with five arches at both ends, hip tile roof, oval pool with coping and fence, two shuffleboard courts, flagpole, lamps and grills. Sizes approximate.

      Corridor interior: from listing photos (carpet, textured walls, paneled unit doors, wall sconces, arched end). Stairs are not shown in any drawing and are not modeled.

      Field repairs: Bldg 100 elevations from the CompanyCam report of Nov 11, 2026 (positions approximate). Bldg 100 balcony floors BF-119 to BF-129 from the CompanyCam report “BALCONIES” (Oct 20–21, 2026), with patch sizes ±6″.

      Heights: estimated from photos (9′-4″ floor to floor, 18′-8″ eave, 22′-4″ roof). A tape-measure photo would make them exact.
